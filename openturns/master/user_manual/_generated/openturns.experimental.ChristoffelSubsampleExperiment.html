(Source code, svg)

class ChristoffelSubsampleExperiment(*args)¶

Christoffel subsample experiment.

Warning

This class is experimental and likely to be modified in future releases. To use it, import the openturns.experimental submodule.

Given an orthonormal basis of and a target size , the experiment builds the first basis functions and returns a weighted design of points. The points are drawn from the Christoffel distribution, or from the determinantal point process (DPP) associated with its kernel. In the fresh mode, the weights are the density ratios of the reference measure over the Christoffel distribution, so the weighted design is an unbiased quadrature rule for . See Christoffel distribution and sampling for the theory of the Christoffel distribution, of DPPs, and of the frame certificate.

Two sizing modes are available.

By default, is the largest dimension supplied by the basis: with an inexhaustible basis, and is tried until the basis supplies functions. For , a fresh design is an exact projection DPP of size associated with the Christoffel kernel. For , the first points are a projection DPP and the remaining points are independent draws from the ChristoffelDistribution; the design is then randomly permuted. See Determinantal point processes on the Christoffel kernel for the exact sequential DPP sampler and its cost.

When the ChristoffelSubsampleExperiment-Certified key is true, the experiment uses the certified sizing of Cohen and Dolbeault [cohendolbeault2020]: is the largest integer with , where is the ChristoffelSubsampleExperiment-Gamma key value, and a fresh design is independent draws from the ChristoffelDistribution. No thinning is applied. The weighted design is checked against the frame bounds; if it misses them, the whole independent sample is redrawn, up to ChristoffelSubsampleExperiment-MaxAttempts draws. If no draw passes, the draw with the largest smallest eigenvalue of the weighted Gramian (the sharpest realized frame) is returned with a warning. This mode makes much smaller than and is not the default; when the key is false, is chosen by the basis capacity as described above. See Weighted least squares and frame certificate for the certified budget and the frame condition.

This certified sizing fixes and adapts . It is natural when the approximation space belongs to a nested hierarchy, but not when is fixed by the model and only can be chosen. In that fixed-space setting the certificate gives the lower bound for the fixed ; the current implementation does not expose a fixed- strategy, which is left for a future release. As a workaround, choose the target size for (and for ), provided the basis supplies functions and . For small the formula can give , in which case the certified space dimension is bounded by ; check getSpaceDimension() and increase (or ) until it is .

When points are supplied to generateWithWeights(), an empty sample of any dimension selects the fresh path. Supplied candidates are assumed to be draws from the reference measure. If more than points are supplied, exactly of them are selected by exact volume sampling, with probability proportional to the determinant of the Gram matrix of their basis features; no fresh point is drawn. When this selection is the projection-DPP law restricted to the pool, and when it is a discrete volume-sampling law. In both cases, unlike a fresh design, the finite-pool law does not inherit the continuous Christoffel marginals, so the returned ratios are not guaranteed unbiased quadrature weights for . If exactly points are supplied, all of them are kept with unit weights, since no Christoffel selection takes place. If fewer than and at most points are supplied, all of them are kept and the DPP is conditioned on their feature vectors; the remaining DPP points are drawn, followed by independent Christoffel points when . Supplying more than points while asking for fewer than points is rejected, as is a set of supplied features that is not linearly independent. See Conditional DPP and volume sampling for conditional DPPs and volume sampling. In certified mode, supplied points are rejected because the certificate assumes independent Christoffel draws; only the fresh path is available.

Each design point of a fresh, conditional or volume-sampled design carries a weight proportional to , the density ratio of the reference measure over the Christoffel distribution. The exactly- supplied design is the exception: it is returned unchanged with unit weights because no selection takes place. The returned weights are positive and finite: a point with a null or non-finite importance ratio is rejected instead of being returned with a zero weight.

For a Christoffel-weighted design, the implementation checks the eigenvalues of the normalized weighted design Gramian against the band , where is the ChristoffelSubsampleExperiment-FrameTolerance key, and warns when they are missed. In the default mode this check is advisory: it is not a certificate that the frame bounds hold. For the design is square and the projection DPP has no certified frame guarantee; for the extra independent Christoffel points improve the budget without making the certificate exact. In certified mode, the independent Christoffel budget is the leading-order Cohen-Dolbeault setting; the omitted factor sets the confidence level, and the realized design is checked. The draw is repeated until it passes, up to the configured attempt limit. If no draw passes, the draw with the largest smallest Gram eigenvalue is returned with a warning. The conditional quasi-optimality of the weighted least squares estimator on the frame event is analyzed in [haberstich2022]. Use computeDesignEigenvalues() when a frame diagnostic is required on a given design. See Weighted least squares and frame certificate for the frame condition and the certified budget.

Notes

A default-constructed experiment has no configured basis: call setOrthogonalBasis() (and optionally setSize()) before generating. Generation from the unconfigured default state throws.

The following ResourceMap keys are used:

  • ChristoffelSubsampleExperiment-FrameTolerance (Scalar, default: 0.5): half-width of the accepted frame eigenvalue band around 1. It must be non-negative and finite.

  • ChristoffelSubsampleExperiment-Certified (Bool, default: False): when true, select the certified sizing and independent Christoffel draws.

  • ChristoffelSubsampleExperiment-Gamma (Scalar, default: 9.242343873386666): constant of the certified sizing. It must be finite and strictly positive when the certified mode is selected.

  • ChristoffelSubsampleExperiment-MaxAttempts (UnsignedInteger, default: 10): maximum number of independent certified draws tried before the draw with the largest smallest Gram eigenvalue is returned with a frame warning.

  • ChristoffelSubsampleExperiment-MaxProjectionAttempts (UnsignedInteger, default: 1000000): maximum number of Christoffel proposals tried to draw one admissible projection DPP point before an error is raised. It only guards against a pathological numerical failure.

Examples

>>> import openturns as ot
>>> import openturns.experimental as otexp
>>> ot.RandomGenerator.SetSeed(0)
>>> factory = ot.OrthogonalProductPolynomialFactory([ot.Uniform(-1.0, 1.0)])
>>> basis = ot.OrthogonalBasis(factory)
>>> experiment = otexp.ChristoffelSubsampleExperiment(basis, 60)
>>> print(experiment.getSpaceDimension())
60
>>> sample, weights = experiment.generateWithWeights()
>>> print(len(sample), len(weights))
60 60

generateWithWeights(*args)¶

Generate the weighted design.

Parameters:
suppliedSample2-d sequence of float, optional

Supplied candidate points, assumed to be draws from the reference measure, e.g. already evaluated points to recycle. An empty sample, whatever its dimension, selects the fresh path. With more than size points, exactly size of them are selected by exact volume sampling. With exactly size points, all of them are returned unchanged with unit weights. With fewer than size and at most spaceDimension points, all of them are kept and the design is completed by a conditional projection DPP, plus independent Christoffel points when size exceeds the space dimension. More than spaceDimension supplied points while asking for fewer than size points is rejected. In certified mode, a non-empty supplied sample is rejected: only independent Christoffel draws carry the certificate.

Returns:
sampleSample

The design of size points.

weightsPoint

The associated importance weights.
