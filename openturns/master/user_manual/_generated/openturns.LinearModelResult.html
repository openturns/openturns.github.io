LinearModelResult¶

class LinearModelResult(*args)¶

Result of a LinearModelAlgorithm.

The quality indicators of the fit, such as the coefficient of determination, are built from unnormalized empirical moments of the output sample, and this class does not accept weights. They are therefore implicitly restricted to output samples obtained with uniform weights, e.g. Monte Carlo simulation: a sample coming from a quadrature rule leads to mis-scaled sums of squares.

LinearModelAlgorithm

Notes

The following ResourceMap key is used:

  • LinearModelAlgorithm-DecompositionMethod (String, default: QR): name of the decomposition method used to solve the least squares problem. The possible values are SVD, Cholesky and QR

  • LinearModelResult-DecompositionMethod (String, default: QR): name of the decomposition method used to solve the least squares problem. The possible values are SVD, Cholesky and QR
