pStudent2D¶

pStudent2D(*args)¶

Rectangular probability of a 2-d Student distribution.

Parameters:
nufloat,

The parameter.

a1, a2float

Lower bounds.

b1, b2float

Upper bounds.

rhofloat

Correlation coefficient.

Returns:
pfloat

The probability

Examples

>>> import openturns as ot
>>> p = ot.DistFunc.pStudent2D(2.5, -0.5, -0.5, 0.5, 0.5, 0.5)
