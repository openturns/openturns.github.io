SO3Manifold¶

(Source code, svg)

class SO3Manifold(*args)¶

Rotation group manifold.

Warning

This class is experimental and likely to be modified in future releases. To use it, import the openturns.experimental submodule.

The special orthogonal group of 3D rotations, embedded in as flattened row-major 3x3 matrices (ambient dimension 9, intrinsic dimension 3). This layout matches the points of the MatrixFisher distribution.

The Riemannian metric is the bi-invariant metric induced by the ambient Frobenius product on : the tangent basis is Frobenius-orthonormal, the geodesic distance between two rotations is times the angle of the relative rotation and the total volume is . The exponential and logarithmic maps are available in closed form through the Rodrigues formulas (Rodrigues, O. (1840). Des lois geometriques qui regissent les deplacements d’un systeme solide dans l’espace, et de la variation des coordonnees provenant de ces deplacements consideres independamment des causes qui peuvent les produire. Journal de Mathematiques Pures et Appliquees, 5, 380-440: rotation of a vector about an axis by an angle expressed with the skew-symmetric matrix exponential. Murray, R. M., Li, Z., Sastry, S. S. (1994). A Mathematical Introduction to Robotic Manipulation. CRC Press, Chapter 2: exponential and logarithmic maps on SO(3) and their axis-angle parametrization used here.

getExpMapVolumeDensity(coordinates)¶

Volume density of the pushed-forward Lebesgue measure.

The exponential map pushes the Lebesgue measure of the intrinsic coordinate space onto the manifold; this density is its Radon-Nikodym derivative with respect to the Riemannian volume, evaluated at the given intrinsic coordinates in the getTangentBasis() basis.

Parameters:
coordinatessequence of float

Intrinsic coordinates in the tangent basis.

Returns:
