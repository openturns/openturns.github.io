CorrectedLeaveOneOut¶

class CorrectedLeaveOneOut(*args)¶

Corrected leave-one-out model selection score for uniform weights.

This score is only defined for uniform weights: any other weight pattern raises an exception, use LeaveOneOut or KFold for weighted designs.

The leave-one-out error is the PRESS statistic (Allen, 1974): with the least-squares fit , the residual and the leverage returned by getHDiag(),

relative to the unbiased output sample variance. It is multiplied by the small-sample correction factor of [chapelle2002] used in [blatman2011], with . This correction has no extension to non-uniform weights, hence the uniform-only restriction.
