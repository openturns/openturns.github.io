pStudentND¶

pStudentND(*args)¶

Rectangular probability of a N-dimensional Student distribution.

Parameters:
asequence of float

Lower bound.

bsequence of float

Upper bound.

musequence of float

Mean vector.

LTriangularMatrix

Cholesky factor of the covariance matrix.

nufloat

Degrees of freedom, .

nint, optional

Number of Sobol’ points used in the QMC integration. Default value is given by the ‘Genz-DefaultSampleSize’ key in ResourceMap.

Returns:
pfloat

The probability .

Notes

This method uses the Genz algorithm, a quasi-Monte Carlo integration based on Sobol’ sequences to compute the rectangular probability of a multivariate Student distribution. The distribution is centered with unit variance.

Examples

>>> import openturns as ot
>>> L = ot.TriangularMatrix(1)
>>> L[0, 0] = 1.0
>>> p = ot.DistFunc.pStudentND([-1.0], [1.0], L, 5.0)
