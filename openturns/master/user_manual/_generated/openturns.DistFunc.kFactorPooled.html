kFactorPooled¶

kFactorPooled(n, m, p, alpha)¶

Exact margin factor for bilateral covering interval of pooled Normal samples.

Parameters:
nint

The size of the sample.

mint

The size of the pool.

pfloat

The probability level of the covering interval.

alphafloat

The confidence level of the covering interval.

Returns:
kfloat

The margin factor

Notes

This method allows one to compute the exact margin factor of a pool of Normal samples of size with unknown means and unknown common variance . This implements the standard NF ISO 16269-6.
