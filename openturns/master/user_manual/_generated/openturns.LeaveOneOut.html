LeaveOneOut¶

class LeaveOneOut(*args)¶

Leave-one-out model selection score.

The score is the weight-mass normalized PRESS statistic (Allen, 1974): with the weighted least-squares fit , the residual and the weighted leverage with returned by getHDiag(),

relative to the weight-mass normalized output variance around the weighted mean. Dropping an observation is a rank-one downdate of the weighted normal equations, so the score is exact for any weights, uniform or not. Unlike CorrectedLeaveOneOut, no small-sample correction factor is applied.
